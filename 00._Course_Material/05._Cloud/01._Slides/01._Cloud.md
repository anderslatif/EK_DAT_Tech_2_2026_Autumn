# Cloud Computing

- Delivery of computing services over the internet (the cloud)
- Includes servers, storage, databases, networking, software, and analytics
- Provides flexibility, scalability, and cost-effectiveness
- Pay-as-you-go pricing model

---

# Types of Cloud Services

```bash
|-----------------------------|
| SaaS: You use the app       | (Google Docs, Microsoft 365, Dropbox etc.)
|-----------------------------|
| PaaS: You deploy code       | (Microsoft Azure App Service, DigitalOcean App Platform)
|-----------------------------|
| IaaS: You manage the OS     | (Amazon EC2, Google Compute Engine, DigitalOcean Droplets, Azure Virtual Machines)
|-----------------------------|
| On-prem: You manage all     | (Your own hardware at your location)
|-----------------------------|
```

*What are the pros and cons of each?*

---

# Managed vs. Self-managed

Managed services alleviate the burden of managing infrastructure, allowing you to focus on your applications and business logic.

But leave you with less control and debugging ability.
