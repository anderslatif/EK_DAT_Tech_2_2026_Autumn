# Exam Preperation

I won't be present on the day, but the classroom is booked. 

This in-class exercise is optional but highly encouraged, as it's good practice for the exam.

---

# Plan

## Round 1

1. Form groups of 3.

2. Use the [Random.org](https://www.random.org/lists/) to assign a different exam topic to each person.

3. Everyone gets 40 minutes to prepare their topic.

4. Each person presents their topic for ~8 minutes. That's shorter than the exam, but it's a good stepping stone. Remember to demo things as well.

5. After each presentation, the other two give feedback:
    - What would you leave out?
    - What would you add?
    - What would you put in a different order?
    - What would you focus on more / less?

6. Take a 10-minute break.

## Round 2

1. Each person picks a new topic that wasn't covered in Round 1. Either choose freely or keep randomizing until you get an uncovered topic. In groups of 3, all but 1 topic will be covered.

2. Same format as Round 1: 40 minutes to prepare, about 8 minutes to present followed by feedback.
