# [Optional] Try out a VPN

VPNs are a way to create a secure connection between your device and another network over the internet. They can be used to protect your data, hide your IP address, and access restricted content.

Many companies only allow their employees to access their internal network through a VPN. This is because the VPN encrypts the data sent between your device and the company's network, making it much harder for anyone to intercept or tamper with it.

VPNs can also be used for private browsing to bypass geographic restrictions on content or access websites that are blocked in your region / by your ISP.
