# About this Course: **Tech 2**

Be on time. I value punctuality highly.

---

## Course Overview

https://katalog.kea.dk/course/3050352

**Workload**: 10 ECTS (14 hours per week - 3 hours lectures w/ exercises, 11 hours of self-study)

---

### Communication

Use Teams to ask questions. Especially if it's something that many might be struggling with.

Going to Code Lab for help is also a great idea.

---

### Mandatories

Prerequisite to go to the exam.

1. **Mandatory I**: Cross-disciplinary work (Projekt #1)

2. **Mandatory II**: Proof of solved exercises. (Do not delete the repositories created to solve the exercises in this course. You just need to link to them to solve this mandatory)

---

### Exam

Read about the exam [here](https://katalog.kea.dk/course/3050352).

> Mundtlig eksamen i 20 min inkl. votering, ud fra spørgsmål i læringsmålene.

Based on the 7 weeks, there will be 7 types of questions that you can draw. For each category, there will be sub-questions.

You will know the categories and sub questions in advance.

Emphasis on doing things live rather than rattling off the theory.
