# Mandatory I

**Type**: Individual.

It would make sense to send in the same URL as the group but the hand-in is individual.

**Hand-in**: Teams Assignment. Hand in a URL. Either raw or in a document.

**Late-Hand-ins**: I will create a thread in the Teams room called Assignments. I will respond if the link works when I check it.

**Feedback**: I will look at whether the link works and serves a project or not. 

**Exam eligibility**: Mandatory I is a requirement to be eligible for the exam. I will create a document to show who has submitted a working link, who has submitted a broken link and who hasn't submitted anything.

---

# Deploy as soon as possible

Do not wait until the last second to deploy.

A good practice is to deploy early and redeploy multiple times throughout the project.

If you have a working link early on, then submit it early. My role is to assess that you have managed to deploy your project, not the content.
