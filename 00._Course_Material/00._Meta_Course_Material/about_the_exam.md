# Tech 2 Exam

Read about the exam [here](https://katalog.kea.dk/course/3050352).

> Mundtlig eksamen i 20 min inkl. votering, ud fra spørgsmål i læringsmålene.

Based on the 7 weeks, there will be 7 types of questions that you can draw. For each category, there will be sub-questions.

You can find the categories and sub questions in the seemster plan in the rightmost column. You are allowed to deviate from the order and add additional elements as long as you cover all the topics. 

Emphasis on doing things live rather than rattling off the theory.

---

# Rules

No notes allowed. You are allowed to prepare screenshots for the questions that call for it.

You are not allowed to have autocompletion in the Terminal (where it shows you the entire command based on your history). Tab-completion is allowed. In case you can’t turn it off, then you will be asked to write the commands in a plain-text document.
