## Requirements

If you are struggling then an HTTP URL will suffice but try to also deploy over HTTPS. But knowing how to use Certbox and signing certificates will be useful in your careers.

Just like everything else in this course, you are neither required to use Azure, nor `one.com`. You are free to use another registrar and any other cloud provider as long as you deploy to a Virtual Machine.
